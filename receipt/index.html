How a $0 probe killed a $400 spend

A worked example of the whole system, receipts included

The idea

A subscription tool promising agencies that their connected social accounts never silently disconnect - reusing an OAuth token vault already built, priced ~$99-149/mo. A ten-seat AI council ranked it #1 of five candidates, on one load-bearing claim: the buyer googles this pain, so search ads replace a sales team.

The kill criterion - written by the board itself

"The disconnect keyword cluster can't supply that traffic - no volume means no-outreach can't reach this buyer."

The board proposed $300-400 of exact-match search ads to test it over two weeks.

The evidence - retrieved in an hour, for free

Keyword reality check — 016's distribution thesis

Run 2026-08-31. Method: Google autocomplete (suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search), which only suggests queries carrying real popularity. Free, no key, repeatable.

Why this probe

The 015 board ranked 016 #1 on one load-bearing claim: the buyer searches the pain, so exact-intent ads replace the customer outreach the founder has banned. Its own tier-2 lenses then split on whether that search event exists. Distribution said she never types "token"; JTBD assumed she googles "instagram token keeps disconnecting" at 11pm. That is a factual question, and the board wanted $300-400 spent to answer it.

Head term instagram token autocompletes to: token api, token debugger, token grabber, token expiration time, token for website. That is a developer cluster. Developers do not buy reconnect insurance; they write the refresh call.

The consumer cluster that does exist ("instagram keeps logging me out") is app users, and is contaminated with AirPods, Insta360 cameras and Beeper. Not agencies.

Verdict

Distribution-lens was right and more precisely than it argued: it is not that the buyer says "logged me out" instead of "token" — the commercial version of this search does not exist at all. Nobody shops for this. They ask Meta to fix it for free.

This fires 015's kill criterion #2 verbatim: "The disconnect keyword cluster can't supply that traffic - no volume means no-outreach can't reach this buyer."

016 is therefore dead under the no-outreach constraint. It is not dead in general: with founder-led outreach to 60-78 agencies it remains arguable, and Porter's supplier-is-substitute objection would still be unanswered. Both prior boards said distribution was the binding constraint. This is that constraint, measured.

Caveat

Empty autocomplete means below the suggestion threshold, not literally zero searches. The evidence is the asymmetry under an identical probe: 8 vs 0.

The decision

KILL 016 pre-spend. The disconnect keyword cluster doesn't exist commercially - the probe returned zero commercial-intent volume - so the no-outreach channel that justified ranking it #1 isn't there. Falling back to 005.
